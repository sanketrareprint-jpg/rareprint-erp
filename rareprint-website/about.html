About RarePrint

Printing excellence since 2010 — trusted by thousands of businesses

Our Story

RarePrint started with a simple vision — to make professional-quality printing accessible to every business, big or small. Founded in 2010 in Mumbai, we began as a small offset press and have grown into a full-service printing house handling digital, offset, and specialty print requirements.

Over the years, we've served startups, SMEs, hospitals, schools, and large corporates — delivering everything from simple visiting cards to complex branded packaging. Our commitment to quality and on-time delivery has made us the go-to printing partner for businesses across Maharashtra.

Today, RarePrint operates with state-of-the-art digital and offset presses, a trained team of designers and press operators, and a customer-first approach that ensures every order meets the highest standard.
